# 15 — Comparison with Traditional Central Banking

## 15.1 Overview

This document provides a detailed, honest comparison between AV Treasury
and traditional central banking institutions. It avoids both the hype of
"blockchain will replace banks" and the dismissal of "this is just a
DeFi protocol."

## 15.6 Effectiveness Comparison

### 15.6.1 Strengths of Traditional CBs
1. **Scale:** Can deploy trillions in liquidity
2. **Legal authority:** Can mandate participation
3. **Lender of last resort:** Can lend against any collateral
4. **Fiscal coordination:** Can work with treasury
5. **Track record:** 100+ years of (imperfect) operation

### 15.6.2 Strengths of AV Treasury
1. **Speed:** Policy updates in 5 days vs 6 weeks
2. **Transparency:** Real-time vs quarterly
3. **Access:** Permissionless vs jurisdiction-bound
4. **Credibility:** Code-enforced vs promise-based
5. **Composability:** Building block vs siloed system
6. **Reduced corruption:** Algorithmic vs discretionary

### 15.6.3 Honest Assessment

AV Treasury is NOT a replacement for the Federal Reserve. It is a
**complement** — a parallel system that serves a different population
(users who want permissionless, transparent, rules-based monetary policy).

The question is whether there is demand for such a system. The growth
of stablecoins ($150B+) suggests there is.

## 15.9 Conclusion

AV Treasury occupies a unique position: it is the first protocol to
implement all three central banking functions in a fully decentralized,
algorithmic manner. It is not as powerful as traditional central banks,
but it is faster, more transparent, and more accessible.

The long-term thesis is not that AV Treasury replaces the Fed, but that
it demonstrates a **viable alternative model** for monetary policy — one
that is rules-based, transparent, and global by default.
